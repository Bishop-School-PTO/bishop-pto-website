About the PTO

Who we are, the 2026–2027 board, and our meeting minutes and newsletters.

Who We Are

The Bishop School Parent Teacher Organization (PTO) is a group of parents, teachers and community members that strive to enrich our children's educational experience and build community spirit at Bishop. We accomplish this by funding events and activities throughout the year and by supplementing resources available to the school and our teachers.

The Bishop community was very generous last year and the PTO raised about $66,000 last school year! This money was used to fund school-wide and grade-level enrichment programs, Bishop classrooms, teachers and administration, school improvement projects, and other community initiatives such as the Bishop garden.

Recording Secretary

Open seat (two-year term)

The Recording Secretary board seat is empty, and we are looking to fill it. Questions and comments can be sent to board@bishopschoolpto.com.

The Recording Secretary serves a two-year term. The Recording Secretary shall keep all records of the PTO, take and record minutes, motions for their approval at the next meeting, and arrange to have them posted on the school website. The Recording Secretary also keeps a copy of the bylaws, rules, and any other necessary supplies, and brings them to meetings.
